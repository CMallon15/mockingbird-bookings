How shows work here

Deal60% to the artist, 40% to the venue, from the cover after a $250 production cost. No guarantees.
Draw we expect40–60 per band Sun–Thu. 75–120 per band Fri–Sat.
TicketsSold through TuneHatch. You'll need a TuneHatch profile to get presale money.
PromoShared. Mockingbird's marketing team promotes the show, and you create and co-host the Facebook event 3 weeks out.
Show dayLoad-in 4 PM, sound check 5 PM (last band first).
PaymentVenmo or PayPal within 2 business days. W-9 required.

Promo plan, both sides

Shows sell when we both post. Here's who does what, with dates worked out from your show date.

    Show night

    Load-in4 PM unless we've agreed otherwise, through the side or back door by the stage.
    Sound check5 PM, last band first. Miss it and you'll get a quick line check only.
    Set timesSet by the venue before the show. Multiple sets: 15–20 minute breaks.
    Tech questionsalex@mockingbirdtheater.com

    Parking during construction

    Follow the gravel driveway behind the theater to the back or side stage door. Unload, then move your vehicle to a nearby lot right away. Cars and buses can't stay parked in the driveway, and the factory may tow vehicles that aren't moved. Please stay off the grass.

    If more than one band is playing, set up with as much gear ready to go as possible so changeovers stay short.

    Parking during construction

    Follow the gravel driveway behind the theater to the back or side stage door. Unload, then move your vehicle to a nearby lot right away. Cars and buses can't stay parked in the driveway, and the factory may tow vehicles that aren't moved. Please stay off the grass.

    If more than one band is playing, set up with as much gear ready to go as possible so changeovers stay short.
